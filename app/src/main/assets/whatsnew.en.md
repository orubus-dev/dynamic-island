### Notifications live on the island
An app notification joins the stack as soon as its banner appears: when the banner leaves, the island keeps the last app's icon with a red badge counting every notification.

### The whole list, one tap
Tap the right half of the capsule to open every notification — icon, sender, text. Tap a row to open the app, or Clear all to remove everything. Tap the icon to open the last one.

### Swipes that don't wreck things
Swiping sideways flips through the stack, up or down just springs back. The stack can no longer be swiped away by accident — the island never disappears.

### Popups stop eating each other
The duplicate shield compares message text: the same notification with new text still shows. The popup queue grew from 6 to 24 — message bursts don't get lost.
