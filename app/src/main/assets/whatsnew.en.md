### Eight languages
Spanish, French, German, Portuguese and Arabic join Russian and English. On first launch the island asks for your language first — System, Русский, English, Español, Français, Deutsch, Português, العربية — and you can change it anytime in Features → Language. Dates and units follow the island's language.

### Self-heal via Wireless debugging
Android 11+: pair with the six-digit code right inside the island, like LADB but without extra apps. One pairing and the island gets WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS — from then on it revives itself after any crash. No computer.

### Recovery that actually recovers
After a crash the engine rebinds the notification listener too, so music, messages and timers don't vanish. A successful heal shows a green "Island revived" pill; after a reboot or update the Resurrector receiver rebinds the listener as well.

### Ten new island states
A "States" section in System alerts: VPN, hotspot, Do not disturb, airplane mode, silent and vibrate, battery saver, low battery, roaming, storage low and no SIM. Each has its own toggle, and they only show when nothing more important is on the island.

### Light Liquid Glass
The island's glass follows your theme: milky glass with dark ink, caustics and a dark lens rim in light mode — same refraction and blur as the dark glass. Look in Colors → Light glass: follow the system, always dark or always light.

### Ads for our own products only
A "From Orubus" card on Home — Relay, MANGACA, Payworth, Sol, Buks, Fossil, OmniHub, Gene. No third-party networks: the list lives in the repo, or in your own Supabase project. Hide removes the card for good.

### The X is gone from switches
On some ROMs a cross bled through on unchecked toggles and read as a delete button. The unchecked thumb is now empty — and the payment apps list no longer contains a news app by mistake.
